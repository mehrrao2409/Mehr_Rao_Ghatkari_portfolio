Who I Am

I bring together analysis, execution and creative judgement.

I am a hands-on performance marketer with experience managing campaigns end to end — from understanding the objective, researching audiences and search intent, and building the campaign structure to setup, launch, tracking checks, budget pacing, creative and ad-copy testing, daily optimisation, reporting, and scaling.

My experience spans lead generation, traffic, remarketing, Google Search, Performance Max, Shopping analysis, GA4 reporting, CRM journeys and landing-page recommendations. The work may begin inside an ad platform, but I look at the wider journey — creative, intent, tracking, forms, landing pages, follow-up and the quality of the outcome.

I work with curiosity and accountability: understanding why performance changed, deciding what to test next and turning each result into a stronger learning loop. I am comfortable taking independent ownership while collaborating across creative, sales, operations, content and analytics, and I am actively growing across the wider performance-marketing ecosystem.

Managing and scaling travel campaigns across Meta and Google Ads

Owned campaigns from setup through optimisation and scaling while managing approximately INR 3-3.5 lakh in monthly spend across multiple destinations and audience-intent levels.

+
Context

A travel brand needed consistent, affordable leads across multiple international destinations, with corporate and leisure buyers requiring different messaging and qualification.

Objective

Generate qualified leads and website traffic at a sustainable cost across destinations, while building a repeatable structure that could scale with demand.

Initial signal or problem

Audience intent and seasonal demand varied widely by destination and buyer type, so a single campaign structure would not work across all of them.

Hypothesis

Separating campaigns by destination, funnel stage, and audience intent — and treating corporate and leisure buyers as distinct journeys — would improve lead quality and cost efficiency versus one shared structure.

What I owned
  • Campaign and ad-set structure
  • Audience research and segmentation
  • Keyword and search-term analysis
  • Budget pacing and bid decisions
  • Creative and copy testing
  • Instant Form and WhatsApp lead flows
  • Daily optimisation and reporting
  • Remarketing and scaling decisions
Actions
  • Separated campaigns by destination, offer, audience intent, and funnel stage.
  • Built cold, engaged, and remarketing audience layers.
  • Tested static, video, and carousel formats and refreshed weak creative.
  • Compared ABO and CBO structures to improve budget distribution.
  • Used destination-specific search intent, negative keywords, Search, and Performance Max.
  • Used distinct messaging and qualification logic where corporate and leisure audiences required materially different journeys.

02
Campaign project Google Search, Shopping and Performance Max
Improving search relevance and traffic quality for an e-commerce campaign

Diagnosed a broad-match search relevance problem and rebuilt intent control, exclusions, and messaging to lift click-through quality.

+
Context

An e-commerce fashion campaign running Google Search, Shopping, and Performance Max needed higher-quality traffic rather than more impressions.

Objective

Increase the share of clicks from genuinely interested shoppers and improve search relevance, without relying only on higher bids.

Initial signal or problem

Broad matching was pulling the campaign into irrelevant searches, weakening traffic quality and CTR.

Hypothesis

Tighter intent control, systematic query exclusions, and more relevant ad messaging would increase the share of useful clicks without relying only on higher bids.

Actions
  • Analysed the search-term report and grouped irrelevant intent patterns.
  • Moved from broad exploration toward phrase and exact control where appropriate.
  • Built a negative-keyword system.
  • Revised ad copy and creative relevance.
  • Reviewed Shopping and Performance Max traffic signals alongside Search.
Results
1.98% → 4.57%
Search CTR (+130%)
3,112
Shopping clicks
INR 3.92
Average Shopping CPC

Within the observed Auction Insights set, the campaign recorded a 31.64% absolute top impression rate, compared with 20.57% for Amazon and 22.05% for Myntra. This metric describes how often an impression appeared in the absolute top position; it does not establish overall market rank or prove that the campaign out-ranked those competitors.

Learning

High impression volume is not valuable when query intent is weak. Search-term quality, match-type control, exclusions, and message relevance should be evaluated together.

Next test

Evaluate conversion value by query and product group, then adjust budget toward high-intent, commercially valuable traffic rather than CTR alone.
